Prompt Engineering Layer

Prompt Studio

Every piece of AI-generated content in AptiGenie — concept explanations, questions, feedback, roadmaps, and the live AI Coach — is produced from a reusable prompt template, not written ad hoc.

What am I looking at? Think of this as the app's "engine room." Everywhere else in AptiGenie shows you the result of AI generation — a lesson, a question, a piece of feedback. This page shows the actual instructions that produce that result: the exact text that would be sent to a model like Claude, written using specific techniques (role prompting, structured output, few-shot examples, prompt chaining, iterative refinement). For each one you can flip between an early attempt (V1) and the refined version actually used, read why it changed, and — in the playground at the bottom — fill in a real template with your own inputs to see it come together.
